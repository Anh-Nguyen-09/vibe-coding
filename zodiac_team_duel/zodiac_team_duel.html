How it works
  • Dice: each round both sides roll 7 dice. Every die has 8 faces: the 7 elements and one rainbow Omni face that counts as any element.
  • Flow: pick your team, a coin flip decides who plays first, then each round starts with a dice screen where you roll and may reroll any dice you pick up to 2 times (tap dice, then Reroll) before returning to the table.
  • Costs: a Normal Attack costs 2 dice: 1 of the card's own element (or Omni) plus any 1 other die, and deals 2 Physical damage. An Elemental Skill costs 3 dice: 2 of the card's own element (or Omni) plus any 1 other die, and deals 3 damage of the card's element (Taurus and Virgo have extra effects). Spent dice are gone for the round.
  • Turns: each round both sides get 3 turns, alternating. On your turn tap a move on one of your cards, then tap the enemy card to hit. You have 15 seconds; if time runs out, or you press Skip turn because you have no suitable dice, that turn is lost. The first player swaps every round. After all 6 turns a new round begins with a new dice roll.
  • Reactions: hit an enemy card that already carries a different element. Vaporize (Pyro+Hydro) +1. Melt (Pyro+Cryo) +2. Overloaded (Pyro+Electro) +2. Burning (Pyro+Dendro) +1. Electro-Charged (Electro+Hydro) +1 and 1 piercing damage to the other enemy cards. Superconduct (Electro+Cryo) +1. Quicken (Electro+Dendro) +1. Bloom (Hydro+Dendro) +1. Frozen (Hydro+Cryo) +1 and the card cannot act this round; hitting a frozen card with Pyro or Physical shatters it for +2. Swirl (Anemo on any element) +1 and 1 damage of that element to the other enemy cards. Crystallize (Geo on any element) +1 and a Shield for the attacker.
  • Special skills: Taurus gains 2 Shield. Virgo heals the most hurt ally for 2.
  • Win: knock out all three enemy cards. Every card has 12 HP.
